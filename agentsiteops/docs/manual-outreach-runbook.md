# Manual Outreach Runbook

This runbook turns launch validation into a bounded manual process. It is not an automation system and does not authorize scraping, mass direct messages, hidden tracking, or platform-rule bypassing.

## Objective

Validate whether the USD 29 Fit Review can create qualified interest before pushing the USD 99 Launch Blueprint.

The first evidence target is not traffic. The target is aggregate signal:

- Qualified replies.
- Fit Review sample views.
- Payment-link clicks.
- Confirmed payments.
- Usable intake messages.
- Repeated objections.

## Allowed Channels

Use only manual, account-safe channels:

- Direct replies to public posts where the offer is relevant.
- Manual email to a known contact where contact is permitted.
- Public build-in-public posts from the AgentSiteOps account.
- Relevant community replies when self-promotion is permitted by the community rules.

Do not use automated DMs, bulk comments, purchased lists, scraped emails, cold spam, hidden tracking pixels, or messages that pretend to be personal when they are templated.

## Message Boundary

Every outreach message must follow these limits:

- State the concrete product: USD 29 Fit Review or USD 99 Launch Blueprint.
- Offer the sample deliverable before payment when the prospect is not already qualified.
- Do not promise traffic, rankings, AI citations, revenue, customer response, payment approval, platform safety, or account outcomes.
- Do not frame search-engine or AI-crawler visibility as buyer demand.
- Do not ask for passwords, API keys, private customer lists, bank data, or account access.

## Batch Rule

Run outreach in small batches so the product can be corrected before reputation damage accumulates.

Recommended first batch:

- 20 manual prospects.
- 2 message variants.
- 1 target segment at a time.
- Review after 48 hours or after 20 sent messages, whichever comes first.

## Segment Priority

Start with prospects who can use the deliverable without extra infrastructure:

1. AI automation freelancers with scattered demos and no clear offer page.
2. Technical solo founders with a tool prototype but no first paid offer.
3. No-code builders selling implementation services but lacking a narrow package.
4. Small operators who have a website but cannot explain the first paid service.

Avoid regulated industries unless the project is only about non-regulated packaging and the buyer understands the advice boundary.

## Stop Rules

Pause or rewrite outreach when:

- 20 sent messages produce 0 qualified replies.
- 5 qualified replies ask what the product actually delivers.
- 3 replies object to trust or proof before price.
- Any recipient flags spam, policy concerns, unsafe automation, or misleading claims.
- The strongest objection cannot be answered by the current sample page.

Stop selling the USD 99 blueprint first when the recurring reply is "I need to know if this idea is worth packaging." Route those prospects to the USD 29 Fit Review.

## Tracking Rule

Record only aggregate outcomes in `data/outreach-tracker-template.csv`.

Do not store names emails handles private replies or payment identifiers in the public repo. Keep private customer records in the payment/account system or a separate private file outside the repository.

## Review Rule

After each batch, classify the next action:

- `continue`: qualified replies or payment evidence exists and objections are answerable.
- `rewrite`: people understand the problem but not the deliverable.
- `reprice`: people want the deliverable but repeatedly object to the first payment.
- `narrow_segment`: only one segment responds.
- `pivot_offer`: the market asks for implementation instead of route judgment.
- `stop`: there is no qualified reply and no useful objection pattern.

## Public Build Post Structure

Use public posts to make the operating boundary visible:

1. Problem: AI-capable builders often have tools but no sellable offer.
2. Change made: added USD 29 Fit Review before USD 99 Launch Blueprint.
3. Proof boundary: no traffic, revenue, ranking, AI citation, or response guarantee.
4. Sample: link to the Fit Review sample and Launch Blueprint sample.
5. Ask: request one current site, profile, or offer page for a fit verdict.
