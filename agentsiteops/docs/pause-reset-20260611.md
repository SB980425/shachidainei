# AgentSiteOps Pause Reset And Three-Day Relaunch Plan

Date: 2026-06-11

Status: paused for product reset. Do not continue UI polish, page expansion, paid traffic, or subscription work until the three-day validation plan below starts.

## Why The Previous Logic Drifted

Confirmed drift:

- The project accepted the goal "earn revenue for operating costs" before proving the buyer, purchase trigger, and delivery value.
- The site moved toward UI, pricing, and payment entry before the paid product was validated.
- The initial "website opportunity scoring" offer is mostly a one-time decision aid. That is not enough to justify a subscription for most solo users.
- AI search visibility and indexing were treated as growth leverage, but search data is not the same as commercial demand.
- The current offer mixed three different products: website scoring, AI search visibility, and one-person AI operations. These must be separated.

Product conclusion:

- Website-opportunity scoring should become a free or low-price lead magnet.
- The paid product must be a concrete implementation artifact or service that saves time, reduces execution mistakes, or creates a ready-to-run operating plan.
- Subscription is not the first monetization model. Subscription only becomes valid if the product contains repeat use: saved projects, monthly updates, client workspaces, case library, monitoring, or ongoing implementation support.

## Login And Payment Decision

No login is required for the first three-day revenue test.

Reason:

- A one-time manual service can charge through PayPal payment links plus an intake form.
- A login system adds implementation and trust burden before the product has proven demand.
- Registration is required only if users need saved projects, dashboards, recurring reports, team access, or subscriptions.

Current rule:

- Use no-auth checkout for the first cash test.
- Add Google/email login only after at least one of these is true:
  - Users request saved history or repeated project comparisons.
  - There are paid customers needing a dashboard.
  - The offer becomes a real subscription or multi-client workspace.

External implementation notes:

- PayPal official materials support shareable payment links and embeddable buttons without building a full checkout flow.
- Clerk and Supabase can support future authentication, but they are not required for this first manual-service launch.
- Lemon Squeezy remains useful for checkout/subscription operations, but the current practical channel is PayPal because the PayPal account is available.

## Three-Day Official Launch Cycle

Hard commercial rule:

- If there is no revenue after three days, stop the current one-time scoring/audit direction.
- Do not interpret "no revenue" as "keep polishing the same offer."
- Pivot to a more direct service offer: AI workflow setup, open-source AI tool deployment, or small-business automation delivery.

Secondary interpretation:

- If no payment but at least 3 qualified buyer conversations occur, the problem is likely offer, price, proof, or payment friction.
- If no payment and fewer than 3 qualified replies occur, the problem is likely channel or target user.

### Day 1: Reset And Proof

Goal:

- Replace the vague audit offer with a concrete launch-map offer.

Actions:

- Freeze current website direction.
- Build or draft one public sample deliverable.
- Replace the main paid CTA with "Get an AI Solo Business Launch Map."
- Add a short intake form: current skill, target market, weekly time, existing audience, budget, preferred monetization, country/language, risk flags.
- Add payment path: PayPal link first, formal PayPal button later.
- Add clear delivery terms: manual review, delivery time, refund boundary, no guaranteed traffic or revenue.

Pass condition:

- A visitor can understand what they receive, submit intake details, and pay without needing an account.

### Day 2: Direct Demand Test

Goal:

- Test whether real people want the deliverable.

Actions:

- Create 1 sample report page.
- Create 3 short outreach messages for different targets:
  - indie/solo founder,
  - AI freelancer,
  - small business operator.
- Send or post 30-50 targeted messages/posts.
- Offer a limited free mini diagnosis, then ask for paid upgrade to the full launch map.
- Track replies, payment clicks, objections, and requested alternatives.

Pass condition:

- At least 1 payment or 3 qualified purchase-intent replies.

### Day 3: Close Or Pivot

Goal:

- Convert or stop.

Actions:

- Follow up with all qualified replies.
- Offer one of three clear products:
  - USD 29 quick triage.
  - USD 99 launch map.
  - USD 299 implementation blueprint.
- Record objections exactly.
- Do not build new features unless they remove a proven payment blocker.

Pass condition:

- At least 1 payment.

Pivot condition:

- Zero revenue after three days.

Pivot options:

1. AI workflow setup service for small businesses.
2. Open-source AI tool deployment/testing service.
3. One-person content operations template pack.
4. English SEO tool/case library with free pages and later paid templates.
