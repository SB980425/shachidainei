# AgentSiteOps Fit Review Delivery Template

Use this template only after confirmed payment and usable intake. Keep customer-sensitive details outside the public repo.

## Order Metadata

- Product: Fit Review
- Delivery target: 24 hours after confirmed payment and usable intake
- Payment status: pending | confirmed | refunded
- Intake status: usable | needs clarification | unsafe | blocked
- Verdict: go | narrow | stop

## Verdict

State one verdict only.

- Go: the buyer has enough evidence and execution ability to buy the full Launch Blueprint.
- Narrow: the buyer should reduce the offer, buyer, or proof path before buying the full Launch Blueprint.
- Stop: the buyer should not buy the full Launch Blueprint yet.

## Strongest Blocker

Name the single blocker most likely to make the full blueprint wasteful.

## Minimum Missing Evidence

List the smallest evidence item that would change the verdict.

Examples:

- One public demo.
- One anonymized before/after screenshot.
- Two qualified replies.
- One paid pilot request.
- A non-regulated scope boundary.

## Recommendation

Choose one:

- Buy the full Launch Blueprint now.
- Collect proof first.
- Narrow the buyer or offer.
- Stop this direction.

## Non-Claims

Do not claim:

- Guaranteed traffic.
- Guaranteed ranking.
- Guaranteed AI citation.
- Guaranteed revenue.
- Guaranteed customer response.
- Legal, tax, financial, medical, safety, or regulated professional advice.

## Completion Check

- Verdict is singular.
- Strongest blocker is named.
- Missing evidence is concrete.
- Recommendation does not force an upsell.
- Sensitive data has not been copied into the repo.
