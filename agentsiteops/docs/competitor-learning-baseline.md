# Competitor Learning Baseline

Date: 2026-06-07
Status: active reference

## Purpose

This note captures what AgentSiteOps should learn from AI visibility and SEO platforms without copying their copy, interface, or paid feature set.

## Products Reviewed

| Product | Positioning observed | Useful lesson | AgentSiteOps response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semrush | SEO suite with AI search and content visibility messaging | Traditional SEO users still need dashboards, audits, gap analysis, and recommendations | Keep technical SEO checks and route-level decisions visible before expanding content |
| AthenaHQ | AI search command center with monitoring, recommendations, citation intelligence, and content workflow | Larger teams buy cross-platform monitoring and action prioritization | Stay smaller: publish a practical operating pack for solo builders and small sites |
| Peec AI | AI search analytics for marketing teams | Prompt-level visibility and competitor benchmarking are becoming expected | Track prompt and citation concepts, but avoid claiming live monitoring until data exists |
| Otterly.ai | AI search monitoring and brand visibility tracking | Users want trend monitoring across AI engines | Build review templates first; add automated monitoring only after traffic and budget exist |
| LLM Pulse | Broad AI search presence, sentiment, share of voice, citations, and paid ad tracking | Platforms are expanding from mentions into action recommendations | Position AgentSiteOps as the execution layer for pages, gates, and release checks |
| Scrunch | AI customer experience and AI-ready site positioning | Enterprise messaging focuses on revenue and customer experience | Keep public copy grounded in crawlability, templates, and validation instead of large ROI claims |

## Differentiation Rule

AgentSiteOps should not compete head-on as an AI visibility dashboard in phase one. The defensible wedge is:

1. Score the site opportunity.
2. Freeze the route blueprint.
3. Publish copy-ready templates and gates.
4. Verify technical SEO and search ownership.
5. Review 30-day evidence before scaling.

## Borrowable Patterns

- Clear category language: AI search, AI visibility, citations, prompt tracking, content gaps.
- Action-oriented CTAs: audit, score, benchmark, optimize, review.
- Trust assets: methodology, sources, update cadence, public checks.
- Product ladder: free diagnostic, starter pack, deeper audit, recurring review.

## Do Not Copy

- Interface layouts, screenshots, product names, claims, pricing tables, customer logos, or proprietary scoring formulas.
- Unverified metrics such as traffic lifts, model coverage, or revenue impact.
- Claims that imply AgentSiteOps has live cross-model monitoring before it exists.
